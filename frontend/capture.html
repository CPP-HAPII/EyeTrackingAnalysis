Eye-Tracking Study Consent

This demo uses your webcam and WebGazer to estimate where you look on the page, so it can build an attention heatmap. Your camera feed is processed locally in your browser — no video or images of your face are uploaded or stored. Only anonymous gaze coordinates are saved. You can stop at any time by closing the tab.

Calibration: keep your head still and your eyes fixed on each dot while you click it 5×. Do the corners too.
Check: stare at the dot in the center without moving your head.
